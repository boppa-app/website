# Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 12, 2026

Boppa is a client-side music player. This page explains what data the app
handles and how.

## What Boppa collects

Boppa does not require an account and does not store any personal data
to any Boppa server.

## Media source packages

Boppa fetches its bundled app configuration from [static.boppa.app/iOS-config.json](https://static.boppa.app/iOS-config.json).

## Third party websites

Boppa has no built in content catalog. Playback happens through a media
source, a package that points Boppa at a specific website and runs its own
code and embedded web views on your device. When you use a media source, 
you are interacting with that website directly, including any cookies, 
logins, or data collection it performs. Those interactions are governed by
the third party site's own privacy policy, not Boppa's. Boppa does not 
control, monitor, or receive data from these sites beyond what is needed
to display and play their content.

If a media source requires you to log in, that login happens inside a
web view for that source, the same as it would in a regular browser tab.
Each media source keeps its own cookies and site data, separate from every
other source. Boppa does not see or store your credentials for third party
sites.
